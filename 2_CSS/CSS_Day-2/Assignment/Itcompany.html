IT Company Details

ZOHO

Zoho Corporation is a leading Indian software company that develops cloud-based business applications. Its products help businesses manage sales, finance, human resources, customer support, communication, and project management. Zoho serves millions of users and organizations across more than 150 countries.

TCS

TCS is one of the world's largest IT service companies. It provides software development, cloud computing, artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, and consulting services. TCS operates in over 50 countries and serves many global businesses.

INFOSYS

Infosys is a leading global IT company that provides software development, consulting, cloud computing, and digital transformation services. It has clients in more than 50 countries and is known for innovation and employee training.
