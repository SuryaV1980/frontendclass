02 The Biological Blueprint

How Neuromorphic Chips Work:
The 3 Core Pillars

Three fundamental engineering breakthroughs allow neuromorphic hardware to mimic the brain's unmatched sensory efficiency.

01

Spiking Neural Networks (SNNs)

Traditional computers process continuous, power-draining streams of 1s and 0s at a set clock rate.

Event-Driven Firing: Neuromorphic chips only send brief electrical signals (called spikes) when an event occurs. If nothing is happening, the artificial neuron stays asleep and uses zero power.
Power Mode Event-Triggered
02

Co-Located Processing & Memory

Traditional hardware demands distinct motherboards, RAM sockets, and CPU dies with buses bridging them.

Synaptic Storage: Instead of separate RAM sticks, every artificial synapse remembers previous signals while immediately computing incoming ones without moving data across a bus.
Data Bus Eliminated
03

Massive Parallelism

Traditional CPUs line instructions up in synchronous queues, processing them sequentially.

Mesh Connectivity: Millions of tiny artificial neurons calculate simultaneously across an interconnected mesh network, matching the brain's ability to process sights, sounds, and motion at once.
Concurrency Millions of Cores
